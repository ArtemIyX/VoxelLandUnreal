# 06 — Certify every planned route and final surface

**Outcome:** No planned cave, branch, junction, entrance or shared regional portal is published unless its complete required player route is proven against the final quantized, meshed field, and no unauthorized surface or protected-solid break exists. M5 acceptance requires evidence for both conditions; a plausible centerline or generated mesh is insufficient.

## Baseline, scope and dependencies

Available components include M5RouteTopology, IdealRouteValidation, SegmentCoverage, FeetProposal/FeetChain, SingleChunkRoute, MultiChunkRoute, MultiRouteChecks, ChunkSet, MeshAdapter, FixedMesh/FixedSupport, TriangleSupport, SweptTriangle, FieldSafety and associated authored tests. These are **partial sufficient checks**, not a complete end-to-end certificate. Some require flat/one-plane or integer-coordinate patches, caller-supplied support, complete mesh/protection provenance, zero renderer uncertainty, or reject cross-plane transitions. RouteTopology has distinct structure-only and Unproven full APIs. PlanPreparation does not publish. None of these statements represents a passing build or validation result.

The owner approved the proposal as the implementation basis and requested a configurable player profile: 185 cm total standing height including hemispherical ends, 35 cm radius, 45° maximum slope, 45 cm maximum step and 10 cm clearance margin are the starting defaults. Contact skin, entrance allowances, numeric tolerances and the relationship to the actual movement profile still need an explicit engineering contract. These defaults are not evidence of walkability. Actual character movement/collision is an M11 end-to-end follow-up, not an M5 substitute.

Use [05](05-cave-pocket-generation.md) for one actual final-field path and mask semantics; [04](04-infinite-world-streaming.md) for bounded cross-region closure; [07](07-shared-resource-accounting.md) for leases, work and cancellation. Current user direction removes historical world-format compatibility, but requires current format validation, deterministic CPU results and a data-only server.

## Proof obligations and algorithm decisions

1. Define a versioned certificate input containing exact immutable descriptor/program/provider identity, voxel size, geometry/mesher version, plan and all required region keys, seed, settings, capsule profile, error bounds, permitted entrance volumes and validated protection. The certificate must enumerate the complete finite plan or bounded infinite dependency closure and identify every required route feature, edge, chamber, junction, entrance and portal. A region pair comparison alone is not a global-connectivity proof.
2. Bind baseline and final fields to the same validated generator and plan; construct fields with the production order and same frozen dependencies. Include pre-filter int16 saturation, optional filter, carve/noise/masks and subsequent quantization. Capture all affected 35³ sample sets and halos; compare X/Y/Z overlaps, including negative coordinates. Missing chunks, insufficient upstream domain, stale epochs, provider loss or any differing global sample is Unproven/error.
3. Determine all changed sample-to-cell and cell-to-triangle influence, including the hard-subtraction range outside primitive radii, filter/mesher halo and snap error. Prove a conservative finite cutoff or process the complete bounded domain. Construct actual Surface Nets output with the same chunk ownership, sign rule Q < 0 solid and Q == 0 air, snapped vertex/triangle rules, conversion and origin handling as runtime. Missing, dropped, degenerate, unsupported or nonmanifold geometry cannot be treated as a safe surface.
4. Derive a complete obstacle/support triangle set and prove its provenance and region/chunk boundary ownership. Address actual snapped fractional vertices and renderer/component transforms; M5FixedMesh Q18 and M5FixedSupport are narrow building blocks, not permission to discard precision or uncertainty. Set a documented representable domain and conservative outward error; ambiguous threshold comparisons are Unproven. Route validation must not depend on a caller assertion that supplied triangles are complete.
5. For each continuous route leg, the upright capsule's medial segment sweeps a parallelogram; its Minkowski sum with the inflated sphere encloses the full capsule translation. Use conservative/exact swept capsule–triangle predicates, such as verified interval separating-axis witnesses in M5SweptTriangle, including both endcaps and obstacles between sample stations. Prove every non-support triangle is separated; equality/tangency or an uncertain rounding interval fails closed unless an explicit certified contact rule permits it.
6. Certify support for the *nominal* capsule throughout every interval, not merely a centerline. Verify floor contact patch/normal, nonpenetration of the entire capsule with support triangles, surface continuity and clearance. Prove slope, step/ledge, ceilings, turning envelope, endcaps, junctions and continuous transition between support patches. Do not blanket-exempt a support triangle from obstacle checks. For step transitions require an explicitly represented lift/forward/down sweep within the profile. One-plane/integer rectangular support is only a conservative subset: extend proof to actual supported triangulations and multiple planes; unsupported geometry must be reported, never marked walkable.
7. Prove route coverage by typed, region-qualified features, not labels alone. Check segment geometry, both inside/outside entrance feet anchors, all portal-side anchors/full portal keys, chamber-to-chamber links, every branch, bends, junctions and connectivity. Validate actual crossing against the final surface and the entire permitted opening. Different overlapping portals or IDs must not be conflated. A route crossing chunks/regions needs all owning neighbor triangles and overlap; no unilateral portal publication.
8. Prove roof and protection against final output. Conservatively enumerate every cell volume intersecting required protection/roof, including cells that emit no triangles because they became all air; compare contributing baseline/final samples and all affected mesher neighbors or provide an equivalent proven geometric bound. Explicit protected cells cannot be waived by an entrance. Verify entrances are the only permitted changes across the baseline surface and that pockets cannot invalidate supported routes or protected solids. Cover filter, mesh and conversion effects; equality only at a few sampled corners is not proof.
9. Enforce finite checked memory, candidate, triangle-pair, interval-refinement, region and total-work limits before allocation; use the shared meter and leases from [07](07-shared-resource-accounting.md). Each interval/triangle operation must be bounded and cancellable. On uncertain arithmetic, unsupported geometry, incomplete data, work exhaustion or cancellation, return diagnostic Unproven/appropriate error naming region/feature/stage. Never publish a partial proof or mutate a prior valid output.
10. Tie the completed certificate to exact identity, plan/region-set generation, sample and mesh versions; check token and ownership after every reentrant step and immediately before publishing. Revalidate/invalidate on any density-affecting graph, provider, profile, mask or stage change. No UObject/Slate dereference in worker code.

## Independent tests to author

- Analytic box/plane/capsule oracle: barely fit/one unit too narrow, low ceiling, mid-segment wall with clear endpoints, tangent, endcaps, bend and non-axis-aligned geometry; independently prove expected cases, not the production predicate.
- Upward/downward, degenerate, nonmanifold and overlapping triangles; a support triangle intersecting capsule body; a missing triangle, enclosed hole wholly between stations, noncoplanar seam, step and ceiling. Test just-below/at/above slope and step limits and exact endpoint contacts.
- Build baseline and final sample fields containing an unauthorized surface break, hollow fully-air protected cell, overhang/multiple crossings, intended versus out-of-bounds entrance, pocket breach and filter/quantization-induced passage closure. Assert failure occurs at the intended stage and no old proof is published as current.
- Actual Surface Nets on both sides of X/Y/Z chunk and regional seams, negative positions, snapping, origin shift and precision limits. Corrupt or omit each owner/halo, duplicate indices and compare independent exhaustive triangle scans with any spatial acceleration.
- Exhaust every route and feature: deliberately omit a branch, chamber, entrance outside anchor, junction, portal side or segment; corrupt full portal key; test mismatched neighboring descriptors and cancellation after one successful route.
- Exercise every arithmetic boundary, incomplete/unknown provider, cancellation in sampling, meshing, index and proof loops, work/memory one below/at limit, stale completion and two-world lifecycle isolation.

## Human verification and exit checklist

- [ ] Named fixtures cover every supported route/entrance on final mesh and fail for the adversarial cases; record actual automation command, logs and pass/fail/skip counts.
- [ ] Inspect named seeds in finite and streamed infinite editor and game, including interiors, openings, unusual roofs and protected volumes. Record capsule profile and proof diagnostics.
- [ ] Run and record real cooked reconstruction, dedicated-server process, cross-host/schedule hash comparisons and independent PIE teardown. Editor NullRHI is not evidence of a dedicated-server target.
- [ ] Measure proof cost and memory on named hardware; establish conservative error and budget bounds rather than presenting proposals as measurements.
- [ ] M5 remains incomplete until all intended routes/entrances have a proven certificate and no unauthorized surface break, or the owner explicitly changes acceptance. Do not describe M11 movement, navigation, collision response or cave lighting as implemented by this proof.
